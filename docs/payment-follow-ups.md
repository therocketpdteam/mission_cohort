# Paid registration follow-ups and compact finance cards

The intake task titled `Follow up on payment status` in PAYMENT_FOLLOW_UP represents an unpaid registration. When its registration is PAID, or active receipt/invoice amounts cover its positive total, open or in-progress instances are completed automatically with completedAt. Partial payments leave the task actionable. Repeating reconciliation does not change completed timestamps.

The rule is deliberately scoped to that standard task title and category. Cancellation refund arrangements, linked QuickBooks reviews, disputes, custom finance tasks, roster work, and previously cancelled/completed tasks are not automatically completed by this rule.

Reconciliation runs on payment-record status synchronization, check-proof reconciliation (inside its existing transaction), bulk payment-status updates, and linked QuickBooks payment updates. Registration detail, cohort task lists, the open-task list, and dashboard reads also repair older stale instances. Those read routes can therefore persist task completion; they do not change financial records or send communications. Dashboard counts and task rows are read after repair so they agree. The parameterized PostgreSQL update uses the same collected-amount rule as cohort finance: the greater of receipt totals and invoice paid totals, not their sum. Refunded/cancelled registrations and comped/zero-value amount-derived cases are excluded. Existing indexes on task status/category/registration/cohort and related payment/invoice registration IDs support the scoped update. No migration or separate live repair script is needed.

Registration and cohort registration-detail follow-up displays share the same open-status predicate. Cancelled tasks and already-paid standard payment-status tasks are excluded; other finance follow-ups stay visible.

Payment Documents and Invoice use the shared CollapsibleSectionCard in both registration views. They start closed, have compact headers/content spacing, and preserve native details/summary keyboard behavior. Warning counts remain visible while closed. Document warnings cover missing PO evidence while unpaid and missing received-check proof; invoice warnings cover missing invoices while payable, overdue open balances, overpayments, and QuickBooks sync errors. Paid registrations do not show stale unpaid/missing-invoice warnings. The registration invoice ledger uses the existing shared collected-payment calculation, including PARTIALLY_PAID receipts and avoiding duplicate counting of mirrored invoice amounts. Automatic resolution does not recreate manually completed or cancelled tasks if a payment is later reversed.

Validation uses isolated task/payment fixtures with outbound HTTP forbidden and browser fixtures with all financial/upload APIs mocked. The browser tests cover compact closed height, keyboard opening, warning visibility/clearance, PO row layout, partial/full and replacement check uploads, and narrow mobile panels. No live payments, customer emails, calendar invitations, or invoice documents are modified during local validation.
